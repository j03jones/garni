Garni

Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Garni is a recipe app made by Joseph Jones (Jones Ridge Kitchen, Jonesboro, Arkansas). This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps about you, where it lives, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is ever sold.
  • You can use Garni without an account. Then everything stays on your own device.
  • If you make an account, your recipes and lists are stored so they follow you between devices, and you can delete the account, and everything in it, at any time from Settings.

Without an account

Your starred recipes, meal plan, shopping list, notes, timers, prices, any recipes you add, and small conveniences (recipes you opened lately, your text size, things you often add to the list) are saved only in your browser or in the app on your phone. They never leave the device unless you export a backup or share a recipe yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data deletes them.

With an account

When you sign up, Garni stores:

  • your email address, the name you choose, and an encrypted (hashed) form of your password;
  • the recipes you save to your account, your starred recipes, ratings, notes, cook log, collections, meal plan, shopping list, kitchen list and food prices;
  • photos you choose to add (dishes and a profile photo), shrunk on your phone before upload;
  • if you joined with an invite, which invite code you used; if you belong to a restaurant, which one.
  • if you share a shopping list with your household, the household's name, its list, and who is in it (the others see your first name).
  • if you buy Garni Pro or leave a tip, what you bought, when, and the amount, so it works on all your devices. Garni never sees or stores your card details.

Recipes you share to the Community are shown to other signed-in people once approved, with your name. Everything else is private to you. The administrator can see accounts and recipes in order to approve Community recipes and help people, and nobody else can.

Where it is kept, and who helps

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and photo storage (servers in the United States).
  • Resend delivers Garni's emails: sign-up confirmations, password resets and invites. It receives your email address and the message.
  • Stripe handles payments on the website, if you choose to buy Garni Pro or leave a tip. You pay on Stripe's own page; Stripe tells Garni which account bought what.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website. Like any web host it sees your IP address when the app's files are downloaded.
  • When you ask Garni to import a recipe from a web page, Garni's server fetches that one page for you. The site sees Garni's server, not you.

The fonts, icons and built-in recipes ship with the app, so using it does not contact anyone else.

Voice control and reading aloud

In cook mode you can have steps read aloud and say “next” or “start timer” instead of touching the screen. Both are off until you turn them on, and stop when you leave cook mode. Reading aloud uses your device's own voice. For voice commands your phone or browser turns speech into text with its own speech service (Apple, Google or your browser's maker may process the audio to do that); Garni only looks for a few command words and keeps nothing.

Scanning a printed recipe

When you scan a recipe, the photo is read on your device and never uploaded. The text reader it uses (the open-source Tesseract.js) is downloaded from jsDelivr, a public code host, the first time you scan; jsDelivr sees that request like any website would, but not your photo.

Importing a PDF works the same way: the file is read on your device and never uploaded, using Mozilla's open-source PDF reader (pdf.js), downloaded from jsDelivr the first time you import one.

Requesting an invite

If you ask for an invite, your name, email, what you do and your note are stored so the administrator can reply, and the administrator gets an email about it. Requests that are dismissed can be removed on request.

Deleting your data

Signed in, go to Settings › Account › Delete my account. That permanently deletes your account, your recipes (Community ones too), photos, stars, notes, meal plan and shopping list. Data kept only on your device is deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data.

Children

Garni is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
