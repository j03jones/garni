## Full description (both stores)
Garni is a calm, ad-free kitchen companion. Find something to cook, plan the week, and shop from one tidy list.

FIND SOMETHING TO COOK
• Search more than 27,000 recipes by name or ingredient, with typo-forgiving search
• "What can I make?" finds recipes from what's already in your kitchen
• Fresh picks every day: quick dinners, seasonal favourites, meat-free mains
• Starter packs: ready-made sets of recipes to begin with

COOK WITH IT
• Hands-free cook mode: big steps, the amounts each step needs, and built-in timers
• Say "next" or "start timer" while your hands are busy, or have steps read aloud
• Scale any recipe up or down, and switch between cups and grams
• Rate recipes, keep a cook log and private notes

PLAN AND SHOP
• Plan the week and drag meals between days
• Build grocery cart: the whole week becomes one shopping list, sorted by aisle
• Send your list to Apple Reminders or Google Keep, or share it with your household
• Keep a list of what's in your kitchen so the list skips what you have

MAKE IT YOURS
• Add your own recipes, or import one from a web page with credit to its author
• Make your own version of a recipe in your own words
• Collections, stars and a food-cost calculator for the price of a dish
• A free account keeps your recipes, plans and lists on all your devices

No ads. Your data isn't sold. Garni works offline once it's loaded.
